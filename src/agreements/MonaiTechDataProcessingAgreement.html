1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement" or "DPA") is entered into by and between JY TECH LTD, trading as Monai Tech ("Processor" or "Monai Tech"), and the customer entity that has entered into a subscription agreement or Terms of Service with Monai Tech ("Controller" or "Customer").

This DPA forms an integral part of the Monai Tech Terms of Service and governs Monai Tech's processing of Personal Data on behalf of the Customer, in compliance with:

  • the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR");
  • the Data Protection Act 2018; and
  • where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("EU GDPR") for Customers with EU operations.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • "Processing", "Data Subject", "Controller", and "Processor" have the meanings given in UK GDPR.
  • "Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by Monai Tech to process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.
  • "Applicable Data Protection Law" means all laws and regulations relating to the protection of personal data, including UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and where applicable, EU GDPR.
  • "Open Banking Provider" means a regulated or authorised third-party provider enabling access to bank account data via open banking APIs at the instruction of the Customer and the authorisation of the relevant end user.
  • "Yapily" means (as applicable) Yapily Ltd and/or Yapily Connect Ltd and/or other entities within the Yapily group providing open banking connectivity, embedded authentication journeys and related services.
  • "Bank Data" means Personal Data derived from a bank connection, including transaction records, dates, amounts, currencies, payee/merchant descriptors, transaction reference strings, and (where applicable) account identifiers and balance information.
  • "Bank Connection" means an end-user authorised connection to a bank account established via Yapily (or another Open Banking Provider), which may expire and require periodic re-authorisation (for example, at least every 90 days).

3. Scope and Duration

This DPA applies exclusively to Monai Tech's Processing of Personal Data on behalf of the Customer in connection with the provision of the Monai Tech SaaS platform and related services.

This DPA remains in effect for the duration of the Customer's active subscription and continues until all Personal Data has been returned or deleted as described in Section 10.

4. Subject Matter, Nature, and Purpose of Processing

Monai Tech processes Personal Data as necessary to provide AI-powered financial insight and analytics services to dental practices and other healthcare businesses. This includes:

  • Receiving and parsing uploaded documents (e.g. invoices, payslips, uploaded bank statements).
  • Accessing practice management and accounting data (where authorised).
  • Generating analytics, financial KPIs, benchmarking comparisons, and AI-driven insights.
  • Facilitating user interactions through the AI assistant and automated support features.
  • Maintaining account records, billing, and technical support communications.

Where the Customer (or its authorised users) elects to use the Platform's optional bank aggregation feature, Monai Tech may (via Yapily as a Subprocessor) facilitate the establishment of Bank Connections and retrieve Bank Data solely to provide the Platform's financial analysis, reconciliation support, KPI generation and related features. Use of this feature is optional and requires end-user authentication via Yapily and acceptance of Yapily's end-user terms where presented.

Processing is strictly limited to the purposes outlined above and as instructed by the Customer.

5. Categories of Data Subjects and Personal Data

Data Subjects

  • Dental practice owners, staff, contractors, and associates.
  • Practice managers and financial administrators.
  • Individuals referenced in uploaded or connected financial records (e.g. staff names, payees, suppliers).

Categories of Personal Data

  • Identification and contact details (name, title, email, phone).
  • Financial records, payroll data, transaction summaries, and supplier invoices.
  • Uploaded document contents and metadata.
  • User account credentials and system interaction logs.
  • Where Bank Connections are enabled: Bank Data, including transaction records and related descriptors and (where applicable) account identifiers and balance information.

Monai Tech does not intentionally process special category data (e.g., health data) except where incidentally included within Customer uploads.

6. Obligations of the Processor (Monai Tech)

Monai Tech shall:

  • Process Personal Data solely on documented lawful instructions of the Controller (including as set out in this DPA and the Customer's configuration and use of the Platform).
  • Not use Personal Data for its own purposes or commercial gain.
  • Ensure all personnel with data access are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:
  • AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit;
  • role-based access control and audit logs;
  • secure hosting within UK-based AWS or equivalent ISO 27001-certified infrastructure.
  • Notify the Controller without undue delay (and within 72 hours) upon becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach.
  • Assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject requests and fulfilling obligations under Articles 32–36 UK GDPR.
  • Provide information reasonably required to demonstrate compliance and support audits, subject to confidentiality and reasonable scheduling.

7. AI and Automated Processing

Monai Tech's AI systems process Customer data solely for analytical and operational purposes.

  • Personal Data is used only to generate insights and reports within the Customer's workspace.
  • AI models are not trained using identifiable Customer data.
  • Any data used for system improvement or performance tuning is anonymised or aggregated to prevent re-identification.

8. Subprocessing

Monai Tech may engage Subprocessors for hosting, analytics, document parsing, open banking connectivity, or AI-related processing.

Current Subprocessors may include:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) – cloud hosting (UK region).
  • Stripe Payments UK Ltd – payment processing.
  • OpenAI / Anthropic / Google Cloud AI – AI text and data analysis (via API).
  • Hotjar / Google Analytics 4 – usage analytics (pseudonymised).
  • Yapily (including Yapily Connect where applicable) – Open Banking connectivity and embedded authentication journeys enabling retrieval of Bank Data where the Customer enables this optional feature.

All Subprocessors are bound by written agreements with data protection obligations equivalent to this DPA.

Monai Tech will maintain an up-to-date list of Subprocessors and notify the Controller in advance of any material changes. The Controller may object on reasonable grounds related to data protection.

9. International Transfers

Where Monai Tech or its Subprocessors process data outside the UK, such transfers will be governed by one of the following mechanisms:

  • the UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA) or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs);
  • adequacy decisions recognised by the UK Government; or
  • other lawful mechanisms approved under UK GDPR.

Additional safeguards, such as encryption and pseudonymisation, will be applied where necessary.

10. Data Retention, Return and Deletion

10.1 Controller instructions and Article 28 choice

Upon termination or expiry of the Services, and subject to Section 10.3, Monai Tech shall (at the Controller's choice and upon written request):

  • return Personal Data to the Controller; and/or
  • permanently delete Personal Data (including copies), in each case in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

10.2 Default retention instruction

By entering into this DPA, the Controller instructs Monai Tech to retain Personal Data associated with the Customer's account in a restricted-access archived state for a period of up to three (3) years following termination or account closure unless the Controller requests earlier deletion under Section 10.1. This retention is intended to support account reactivation, continuity of historical analysis, auditability, and dispute resolution.

10.3 Legal hold / mandatory retention

Monai Tech may retain Personal Data to the extent required by applicable law, regulation, or binding order, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Where Monai Tech retains Personal Data under this Section 10.3, it will restrict access and continue to protect the data in accordance with this DPA.

10.4 Deletion timing and confirmation

Where deletion is requested under Section 10.1, Monai Tech will delete the relevant Personal Data within a reasonable period (normally within 90 days) unless a longer period is required due to technical constraints (including backup rotation) or lawful retention under Section 10.3. Monai Tech will provide written confirmation of deletion upon request.

11. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions defined in the main Terms of Service.

Monai Tech shall be liable for the acts and omissions of its Subprocessors to the same extent as for its own actions.

12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This DPA is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
