1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Monai Tech ("we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you access our website and software platform (together, the "Platform").

This Cookie Policy forms part of our Privacy Policy and should be read alongside our Terms of Service, Data Processing Agreement, and Liability Disclaimer.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website or use an online service. Cookies allow systems to recognise your device, maintain sessions, store preferences, and support essential functionality.

Cookies may be:

  • Session cookies – deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies – stored for a defined period
  • First-party cookies – set by Monai Tech
  • Third-party cookies – set by external service providers

3. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Enable core website and Platform functionality
  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions
  • Protect the Platform against unauthorised access or abuse
  • Remember user preferences and settings
  • Analyse aggregated usage patterns to improve performance and usability
  • Support optional third-party integrations when enabled by the user

We do not use cookies to sell personal data or to track users across unrelated websites without consent.

4. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of the Platform and cannot be disabled through our systems.

They are used for:

  • User authentication and session management
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Load balancing and infrastructure stability

Without these cookies, the Platform cannot function correctly.

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the Platform in aggregate so we can improve functionality and user experience.

They collect information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Error and performance metrics

Where required by law, these cookies are deployed only with user consent and are configured to minimise personal data collection.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember user preferences and configuration choices, such as:

  • Language or region
  • Interface preferences (e.g. dark mode)
  • Session continuity

Optional Integration Cookies (Open Banking / Bank Aggregation)

If you choose to enable the optional bank account aggregation feature, additional cookies or similar technologies may be used temporarily as part of the secure authentication flow provided by the third-party open banking provider.

These cookies may be used to:

  • Maintain session continuity during the bank authentication journey
  • Ensure secure redirection between systems
  • Prevent fraud or unauthorised access

These cookies are not controlled directly by Monai Tech and are subject to the third-party provider's own cookie and privacy policies. Monai Tech does not use these cookies to track users beyond the authentication process.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by trusted third-party providers that support Platform functionality, analytics, infrastructure, or optional integrations.

Where third-party cookies are used:

  • They are limited to what is necessary for the relevant service
  • Providers are contractually required to comply with applicable data protection laws
  • Use of optional integrations is initiated by the user

Monai Tech does not permit third-party cookies for unrelated advertising or behavioural profiling without appropriate consent.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Where required by law, you will be given the option to manage non-essential cookies via a cookie consent mechanism when you first visit our website or Platform.

You may also manage cookies through your browser settings, including:

  • Blocking or deleting cookies
  • Receiving alerts when cookies are set

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect Platform functionality or limit access to some features.

7. Cookie Duration and Retention

  • Session cookies are deleted automatically when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies remain for a defined period or until manually deleted.
  • Cookies used solely for security, authentication, or integration flows are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil their function.

Monai Tech periodically reviews cookies in use to ensure continued compliance with legal and technical best practices.
