1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Monai Tech ("Monai Tech," "we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and Platform. It forms part of our Privacy Policy and is designed to help you understand what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognise your device, remember user preferences, improve performance, and support analytics and marketing functions.

Cookies may be:

  • Session cookies – deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies – stored for a set duration or until deleted.
  • First-party cookies – placed by Monai Tech directly.
  • Third-party cookies – placed by service providers (e.g., analytics or marketing partners).

3. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Ensure our website and Platform function correctly.
  • Improve user experience and navigation.
  • Analyse site traffic, usage, and performance.
  • Remember login details and personal preferences.
  • Support marketing, remarketing, and advertising campaigns (where consent is given).
  • Enhance product analytics and AI model performance metrics.

7. Cookie Duration and Retention

Session cookies are deleted automatically when you close your browser.

Persistent cookies remain active for a defined period (usually between 1 month and 2 years) or until manually deleted.
