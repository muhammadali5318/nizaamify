What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website or use an online service. They allow systems to recognise your browser or device and store limited information for a range of operational, functional, analytical, and security purposes.

Cookies may be:

  • session cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser;
  • persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a defined period or until deleted;
  • first-party cookies, which are set by Monai Tech; or
  • third-party cookies, which are set by trusted third-party providers supporting the Platform or its features.

We may also use similar technologies such as local storage, session storage, SDKs, tags, pixels, or authentication tokens where appropriate. For simplicity, this Policy refers to these collectively as "cookies" unless otherwise stated.

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • to enable core website and platform functionality;
  • to authenticate users and maintain secure sessions;
  • to protect the Platform against fraud, abuse, unauthorised access, and other security threats;
  • to remember user settings and preferences;
  • to support platform performance, stability, and troubleshooting;
  • to understand how users interact with the Platform so that we can improve usability, workflows, and features;
  • to support optional third-party integrations where enabled by the user;
  • to comply with legal and regulatory requirements relating to consent, privacy, and platform operation.

We do not use cookies to sell personal data. We do not permit unrelated third-party advertising or behavioural profiling cookies through the Platform without an appropriate lawful basis and, where required, valid consent.
