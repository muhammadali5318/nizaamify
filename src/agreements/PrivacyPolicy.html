1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how JY TECH LTD, trading as Monai Tech ("Monai Tech", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data through our website, software platform, and related services (together, the "Service").

We are committed to protecting personal data and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

By accessing or using the Service, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to our website (www.monai.tech)
  • Registered users of the Monai Tech SaaS platform
  • Business representatives of dental and healthcare practices
  • Individuals whose personal data may appear within uploaded or connected financial records (e.g. staff names on payslips or supplier invoices)

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, banks, or services that you may access through integrations. For example, where you choose to connect a bank account via our open banking partner, your interaction with that provider is governed by their own privacy policy and terms.

3. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

A. Data you provide directly

  • Name, job title, business name, email address, and contact details
  • Account login credentials
  • Uploaded financial and operational documents (e.g. invoices, payslips, statements)

B. Data collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device and operating system information
  • Platform usage data, audit logs, and interaction history
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy)

C. Data received from third parties

  • Payment and subscription details (via Stripe)
  • Data from authorised integrations with practice management, accounting, or financial systems
  • Bank transaction data retrieved via an optional open banking connection (provided through Yapily), where you explicitly authorise the connection. Bank connections expire after 90 days and must be re-authorised by you to remain active.

4. Purposes of Processing

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Monai Tech platform
  • To authenticate users and manage accounts
  • To analyse uploaded or connected financial data and generate insights, KPIs, benchmarks, and reports
  • To retrieve and process bank transaction data where you choose to enable an open banking connection
  • To communicate with users regarding support, product updates, and billing
  • To monitor platform security, prevent fraud, and maintain system integrity
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To improve our products, analytics, and benchmarking capabilities using aggregated and anonymised data

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual necessity – to provide the Service and features you choose to use
  • Legitimate interests – to operate, secure, improve, and protect the platform, support customer reactivation, and defend legal claims
  • Consent – where required (e.g. non-essential cookies or marketing communications)
  • Legal obligation – where processing is required by law or regulation

6. Role of Monai Tech

When providing services to business customers:

  • The Customer acts as the Data Controller
  • Monai Tech acts as a Data Processor, processing personal data on the Customer's behalf in accordance with the Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

7. AI and Automated Processing

Monai Tech uses AI and automated systems to analyse financial data and generate insights.

  • AI models process structured and unstructured financial data to calculate KPIs, trends, and benchmarks
  • Identifiable personal data is not used to train AI models
  • Any data used for system improvement is first aggregated and anonymised
  • AI-generated outputs are indicative only and do not constitute professional advice

8. Data Sharing and Subprocessors

We may share limited data with trusted third-party subprocessors that support our Service, including:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) – cloud hosting (UK/EU regions)
  • Stripe Payments UK Ltd – billing and payments
  • Yapily Ltd – open banking connectivity (optional, user-authorised)
  • OpenAI, Google Cloud AI, Anthropic – document and data analysis via secure APIs
  • Hotjar and Google Analytics 4 – platform usage analytics (pseudonymised)

All subprocessors are subject to contractual data protection obligations equivalent to UK GDPR requirements.

We do not sell or rent personal data.

9. International Data Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards, including:

  • UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA)
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Adequacy decisions recognised by the UK Government
  • Additional technical safeguards such as encryption and access controls

10. Data Retention

Monai Tech retains personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including service provision, customer reactivation, legal compliance, and protection of our legitimate business interests.

Where an account is closed or becomes inactive, Monai Tech may retain customer data for a period of up to three (3) years to:

  • Enable account reactivation without requiring users to re-upload or reconnect data
  • Maintain historical financial analysis and continuity
  • Support auditability and dispute resolution
  • Defend or establish legal claims

Users may request deletion of their personal data at any time, subject to our legal obligations and legitimate interests.

Aggregated, anonymised, or de-identified data that no longer constitutes personal data may be retained indefinitely for benchmarking, analytics, research, and product improvement.

11. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, including:

  • Encryption at rest and in transit
  • Role-based access controls and audit logging
  • Regular infrastructure monitoring and testing
  • Strict staff confidentiality and access restrictions

No system is completely secure, and users are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials.
