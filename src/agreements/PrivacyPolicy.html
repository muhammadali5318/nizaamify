1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how JY TECH LTD, trading as Monai Tech ("Monai Tech," "we," "our," or "us"), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data through our website, platform, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and other applicable privacy laws. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

By using the Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to our website (www.monai.tech)
  • Registered users of the Monai Tech SaaS platform
  • Business representatives of dental and healthcare practices
  • Individuals whose personal data may appear in uploaded documents (e.g. payslips, invoices)

This policy does not apply to third-party websites or services linked to or integrated with Monai Tech.

3. Personal Data We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

A. Data you provide directly:

  • Name, job title, email, phone number, and company/practice details
  • Account login credentials
  • Uploaded financial and operational documents (e.g., invoices, payslips, statements)

B. Data collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, device information, and session data
  • Platform usage analytics and interaction logs
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy)

C. Data received from third parties:

  • Payment and subscription information (via Stripe)
  • Integrations such as PMS or accounting software (where authorised)

4. Purpose of Processing

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Monai Tech platform
  • To authenticate and manage user accounts
  • To analyse uploaded financial documents and generate insights
  • To communicate with users regarding product updates, support, and billing
  • To ensure compliance with legal obligations
  • To protect our systems, detect fraud, and maintain platform security

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we process personal data on the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual necessity – to deliver the Monai Tech Service as agreed with you.
  • Legitimate interest – to operate, secure, and improve our platform.
  • Consent – for non-essential cookies or marketing communications.
  • Legal obligation – to comply with record-keeping, tax, or regulatory duties.

6. Role of Monai Tech

When providing services to business customers (e.g., dental or healthcare practices):

  • The Customer acts as the Data Controller (determines purpose and means of processing).
  • Monai Tech acts as the Data Processor (processes data on the Customer’s behalf) under the terms of the Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

7. AI and Automated Data Processing

Monai Tech uses AI and automation to analyse uploaded data and generate financial insights.

  • AI models process structured and unstructured financial information to calculate KPIs, benchmarks, and trends.
  • Identifiable data is not used to train or fine-tune AI models.
  • Data used for algorithmic improvement is anonymised or aggregated to prevent identification.
  • AI-generated outputs are estimates only and should not be relied upon as professional advice.

8. Data Sharing and Subprocessors

We may share limited data with trusted third-party subprocessors who help us operate the platform. These include:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) – cloud hosting (UK region)
  • Stripe Payments UK Ltd – payment processing
  • OpenAI / Google Cloud AI / Anthropic – document and text analysis via secure APIs
  • Hotjar / Google Analytics 4 – anonymised user analytics

All subprocessors are contractually bound to implement appropriate data protection and security measures in accordance with UK GDPR.

We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.

9. International Data Transfers

Where data is transferred outside the UK, Monai Tech ensures lawful transfer mechanisms such as:

  • The UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA)
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Adequacy decisions approved by the UK Government
  • Additional safeguards such as encryption and pseudonymisation

10. Data Retention

Monai Tech retains personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Service and fulfil contractual or legal obligations.

Upon account closure or termination, uploaded documents and related data are permanently deleted within 90 days, unless retention is required by law.

Aggregated, anonymised analytics may be retained for product improvement.

11. Security Measures

We implement technical and organisational security measures including:

  • AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit
  • Role-based access control and audit logging
  • Regular penetration testing and infrastructure monitoring
  • Strict staff confidentiality and access controls

While we take all reasonable precautions, no system is completely secure. Users are encouraged to maintain strong passwords and restrict access to their accounts.

12. Your Data Protection Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access – request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification – correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Erasure – request deletion of your personal data
  • Restriction – limit how we process your data
  • Portability – receive data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection – object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@monai.tech. We will respond within one month as required by law.

13. Marketing Communications

We may send updates or product information if you have opted in. You can opt out at any time by:

  • Clicking the “unsubscribe” link in an email; or
  • Contacting us directly at hello@monai.tech.

We will not send marketing messages without your consent.

14. Children’s Privacy

The Monai Tech platform is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us immediately for removal.

15. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in law, technology, or our data practices. The latest version will always be available on our website.

Material changes will be communicated to registered users via email or in-app notification.
